Combined matchups

Games are pooled by engine, opening book, board size and depth, and bot settings (difficulty and fixed depth or time limit). The app version is recorded and shown, and does not split a pool. Bot 1 and Bot 2 are the same bot when their settings match. Each config shows wins, losses, and draws as Black and as White. Black always moves first. Fixed-depth pools combine animated and batch games from any machine. Time-limit pools combine animated and batch games that share a CPU benchmark band, and the split is shown. Clock timeouts are saved and left out of these pools.
